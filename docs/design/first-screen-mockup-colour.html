What is new: colour with a reason, and more that reacts

Same scene you picked. Two changes.

Colour theory

  • Blue and orange are opposites on the colour wheel. Put side by side, each makes the other look stronger, so the page looks brighter without getting darker anywhere.
  • Blue is air. Orange is energy. Wind arrives blue. When it passes through a rotor it turns orange and slows down: the turbine took its energy. Then it fades back to blue. The colour change is the physics.
  • Warm light, cool shadows, the painter's rule. The sun is the only warm thing; the far hill, the sky and the turbine's shadow lean blue.
  • The same two colours carry the charts. One scale, blue through white to orange, shows wake loss, and where the model ran under or over the real farm.

New things that react

  • The headline leans. Each letter bends in the wind you make and springs back.
  • Press, hold and let go anywhere to charge a gust and send a ring of wind across the sky.
  • The grass on the ridge bends with the air above it.
  • The sun glows brighter the more the farm is making.
  • Still there: sweeping pushes the air, turbines speed up, pulses run along the ground, chapters fill.
